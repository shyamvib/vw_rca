Read

We lost 3,856 orders worth $2.22M and clawed back $1.19M through higher AOV. Churn and new-logo acquisition net to roughly zero. The whole hole is the top 20% of retained clinics (-$1.84M, 179% of the shortfall, since every other cohort nets positive), and CA / AZ / MO / FL carry ~109% of it.

The four months are evenly spread: 29% / 12% / 36% / 23%. July is the largest single month but no longer the dominant driver, and its mid-month weeks turn flat-to-positive — only the July 4 week and the last week of July are genuinely soft. August is the worrying month, because the damage there is almost entirely bundle orders (-$231K of -$240K) and the AOV cushion has gone.

Caveats

The two worst weeks (May 25, Jun 29) are the Memorial Day and July 4 weeks, and the baseline window contains no comparable holiday. Roughly $400–500K of the gap is calendar, not demand. Strip those out and the underlying run-rate loss is roughly $0.5–0.6M, still entirely volume and still entirely the top-20% cohort.

The AOV cushion (+$55–120K/week through July) has collapsed in August (+$9–15K/week), which is why daily revenue now feels worse than the monthly numbers suggested.
